# Image credits

All images in this folder, and the clips in /public/media, are AI-generated
editorial and product imagery. They illustrate product types and a typical
towel production process. They are NOT photographs of WeaveSources'
manufacturing partners, their facilities or workers, actual inspections,
shipments, certificates or customers' orders, and must not be presented as such.

On the site they are labelled "Illustration", "Illustrative footage" or
"Illustrative product styling". Keep those labels while these files are in use.

Sources: ./content (supplied October 2026), processed by
`node scripts/prepare-media.mjs`. Earlier files (hero.jpg, product-*.jpg for the
original six towels, folded-stack.jpg, sample-pair.jpg, detail-terry.jpg) were
generated from the prompts in docs/photography-brief.md.

Replace with photographs of real samples and (with permission) real partner
facilities under the same file names; then remove the illustration labels for
those specific images only.
